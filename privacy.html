Privacy Policy

Gmail Agent — last updated September 18, 2026

Gmail Agent (“the App”) is a private, personal-use application operated solely by the owner of its Google API credentials (“the operator”).

What the App does

The App accesses the operator's own Google account data (Gmail) through Google APIs — strictly on behalf of, and with the explicit consent of, the operator — to organize their email (search, labeling, archiving).

Data collection and storage

  • The App does not collect, store, or share personal data with the developer or any third party.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally on the operator's device.
  • Email data is processed locally on the operator's device and is not transmitted to servers operated by the App.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. The App communicates only with Google's APIs, as authorized by the operator.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
