Gmail Agent

A private, personal-use application

Gmail Agent is a locally-run tool that connects its operator's own Google account to their personal AI assistant to help organize email — searching, labeling, and archiving — through the Gmail API.

It is not a public product or service. It has a single user (its operator), collects no data, and runs entirely on the operator's own device.
